# UC Design Blueprint

Structured workshop engagement to design and deploy Unity Catalog governance for complex environments (multi-region, multi-cloud, multi-BU, regulated data).

## Ownership

- **Primary owner:** Allan Cao (`allan.cao@databricks.com`)
- **Secondary owner:** Assign a backup maintainer per your Data Governance specialization lead (required by [CONTRIBUTING](../../CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## Purpose and objectives

Simplify and accelerate UC design and deployment via specialist-led working sessions—from requirements through best-practice patterns to a first-pass UC design (~5 weeks for the four-step flow).

## Prerequisites

- L200 enablement complete; discovery done  
- Metastore admin, workspace admin, SCIM, and catalog/connection privileges as needed  
- Cloud/platform, engineering, and security participants available  
- Technical owner familiar with enterprise taxonomy  

See **Technical Readiness Criteria** and **Customer Requirements** in [`catalog-listing.yml`](catalog-listing.yml) (`demo_description`) for the full checklist.

## Workflow

Four joint steps: **Preparation** → **Define & validate requirements** → **UC design patterns review** → **Whiteboard design**, with documented outputs after each phase. Details, timelines, and GTM signals are in the catalog listing description.

## Expected outcomes

- Documented governance requirements and gaps mapped to UC capabilities  
- Best-practice design patterns applied to the customer context  
- Draft UC governance design (diagram / doc) suitable for iteration and sign-off
